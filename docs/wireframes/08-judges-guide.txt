Every other flow asks what you want to buy. This one asks whether your family can carry on without you.

The formulas
Real rate        r = (1 + d) ÷ (1 + g) − 1
Annuity factor   AF = (1 − (1 + r)^−n) ÷ r      (r ≠ 0; AF = n when r ≈ 0)
Annuity due      AF_due = AF × (1 + r)

HLV  = I × (1 − e) × AF        ordinary annuity, n = years to retirement
Need = loans + living cost × AF_due(i) + education
       + marriage + health and emergency + spouse retirement
Gap  = Need − own death cover − employer cover − allocated savings

d discount rate, g income growth, i expense inflation, n years, I income, e own-spending share. Guards: r ≤ −1 rejected; n ≤ 0 gives HLV 0; full precision internally, rounded only for display. PRD §5.10

The rules, in order
ID	WHEN	THEN
R1	Gap > 0	Term first, recommended
R2	Lifelong dependant or estate need, and affordable	Whole life as an add-on
R3	Term gap closed and budget left over	Endowment or ULIP as add-ons
R4	Gap ≤ 0	"You appear adequately covered"; nothing pushed
R5	Premium above affordability cap	Lower cover → change term → drop riders → advisor
R6	Need above insurer limit	Show both; route to advisor; need not capped
E-*	Outside age, term or cover limits	Excluded, with reason and nearest eligible
